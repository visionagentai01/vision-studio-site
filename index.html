VISION Studio

A desktop creator workflow for preparing original short-form video, reviewing every publication setting and publishing to TikTok accounts connected by their owners through TikTok's official APIs.

What VISION Studio does

VISION Studio prepares clips, captions, titles and hashtags locally. Before content is submitted, the creator can review the video preview, edit the caption and hashtags, choose an available privacy level and interaction settings, and expressly confirm the schedule.

Creator control

Only an account owner who completes TikTok authorization can connect an account. VISION Studio never publishes to an unrelated account.

Local media

Video is prepared on the creator's computer and transferred directly to TikTok with FILE_UPLOAD. Credentials remain server-side.

Clear status

Scheduled, processing, published and failed items are shown separately. An upload is not marked published until TikTok confirms it.

Original work

Creators must own their content or have permission to use it. VISION Studio does not remove ownership or platform obligations.
