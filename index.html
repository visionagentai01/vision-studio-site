Creator publishing workflow

VISION Studio

VISION Studio is a desktop application that helps creators turn their own footage into finished vertical clips, review every caption and setting, and publish to the TikTok account they own — using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

See how it worksExplore features

What VISION Studio does

Creators spend most of their time cutting, captioning and re-uploading. VISION Studio runs those steps on the creator's own computer: it proposes clips from a source video, drafts a title, caption, hashtags and subtitles, and presents everything for review. Nothing reaches TikTok until the creator has looked at the preview and expressly confirmed the post or its schedule.

Creator control

Only the owner of a TikTok account can connect it, by completing TikTok's own authorization screen. VISION Studio never publishes to an account that has not been connected by its owner, and every post is confirmed by the creator before it is sent.

Local media

Video files are prepared on the creator's computer and transferred directly to TikTok with the official FILE_UPLOAD method. Access tokens are stored server-side, never in this website or in any public repository.

Transparent status

Scheduled, processing, published and failed items are shown separately in the application. A post is only marked as published once TikTok has confirmed it.

Original work only

Creators must own their content or hold the rights to use it. VISION Studio does not remove the creator's responsibilities under TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Who it is for

VISION Studio is designed for individual creators and small teams who publish their own short-form content and want a reliable, reviewable pipeline from raw footage to a live TikTok post. It is not a bulk-posting service, it does not manage accounts on behalf of third parties, and it does not sell or rent access to TikTok data.
