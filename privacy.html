Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

1. Who we are

VISION Studio ("VISION Studio", "we", "us") is a desktop creator application developed and operated by an independent developer. For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, including the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and the French Data Protection Act (Loi Informatique et Libertés), the operator of VISION Studio is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at measaidi953@gmail.com.

This policy covers the VISION Studio desktop application and this public website. It does not cover TikTok itself, whose processing is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

2. Data we access through TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok asks you to approve specific permissions ("scopes"). VISION Studio requests only the following, and only receives the data they cover:

ScopeData receivedPurpose
user.info.basicOpen ID, union ID, display name, avatarIdentify the connected account in the application and confirm which account will receive a post.
user.info.statsFollower, following, like and video countsDisplay an account overview in the creator's dashboard.
video.listList of the account's public videos (ID, title, cover, statistics)Verify that a publication succeeded and show its status.
video.publishPublish status and publish ID returned by TikTokPublish a creator-approved video and track its processing state.

We also receive an access token and a refresh token from TikTok so that the application can call these APIs on your behalf. We never receive or store your TikTok password.

3. Data you provide to the application

  • Video files and source material you choose to prepare.
  • Titles, captions, hashtags, subtitles, schedules, privacy levels and interaction settings you select.
  • Your email address and message if you contact support.

4. Data this website collects

This website is a static information site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies of its own, contains no analytics or advertising trackers, and has no login. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may record standard server logs (such as IP address and user agent) in accordance with GitHub's privacy statement.

6. Sharing and international transfers

Your data is shared only with:

  • TikTok, which receives the video, caption and publication settings needed to perform the publication you requested, and which processes them under its own policies.
  • Infrastructure providers used to run the VISION Studio back end (hosting) and GitHub (this website). These providers act on our instructions.
  • Authorities where we are legally required to do so.

TikTok and hosting providers may process data outside the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area. Where that happens, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses as required by the UK GDPR and EU GDPR.

7. Storage and security

Access and refresh tokens, prepared media and scheduling data are stored on infrastructure controlled by the operator, protected by access controls and encrypted transport (HTTPS). Secrets are never embedded in this public website or committed to any public source repository. Access is restricted to the operator.

9. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights, email measaidi953@gmail.com; we respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, such as the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) or the French CNIL.

10. Disconnecting and deleting your data

  • In VISION Studio, choose Disconnect TikTok: stored tokens are deleted immediately.
  • On TikTok, go to Settings → Security → Apps and services and remove VISION Studio: TikTok invalidates the tokens.
  • Delete prepared clips and publication history from within the application, or email us to have all data associated with your account erased.

11. Children

VISION Studio is not directed at children. You must meet TikTok's minimum age requirement to hold a TikTok account and must be at least 18 to use VISION Studio. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the application changes or when the law requires it. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision. Material changes will be highlighted inside the application.

13. Contact

Privacy questions, access or deletion requests: measaidi953@gmail.com.
