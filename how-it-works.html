How it works

From a source video to a verified TikTok publication, in seven transparent steps.

  1. Connect your TikTok account. In VISION Studio, choose Connect TikTok. TikTok opens its own authorization page, lists the requested permissions, and you decide whether to approve. VISION Studio never sees your TikTok password.
  2. Prepare. Provide an original video or a source you are authorized to use. VISION Studio keeps the file on your computer.
  3. Understand. VISION Studio analyses the material and proposes clips, subtitles, a title, a caption and relevant hashtags.
  4. Review. You watch the rendered preview and can edit the caption, hashtags, privacy level and interaction settings for each clip.
  5. Confirm. Nothing is transferred until you press Publish or Schedule for that clip.
  6. Upload. The file is sent directly to TikTok using the official Content Posting API (FILE_UPLOAD).
  7. Verify. VISION Studio checks TikTok's processing status and only marks the item as published once TikTok confirms it. The post then appears in your dashboard next to your existing public videos.

Account authorization

VISION Studio requests only the TikTok scopes needed for the features described on this site: user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list and video.publish. Authorization can be revoked at any time by the account owner, either inside VISION Studio or from TikTok's security settings.

Publication limits

Privacy choices, daily posting limits and feature availability are decided by TikTok and may differ for each connected account. VISION Studio honours the options returned by TikTok's creator-info endpoint and never bypasses them.
