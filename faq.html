Frequently asked questions

Does VISION Studio post automatically without me?

No. VISION Studio prepares clips and proposes captions, but every publication or schedule must be confirmed by you for that specific clip. Scheduled posts can be edited or cancelled before they are sent.

Which TikTok account will receive my posts?

Only the account you connected yourself through TikTok's authorization page. VISION Studio shows the display name and avatar of the connected account before you publish.

Does VISION Studio ask for my TikTok password?

Never. Authorization happens on TikTok's own website using Login Kit. VISION Studio only receives an access token, which you can revoke at any time.

Where are my videos stored?

On your own computer. The rendered file is uploaded directly to TikTok when you publish. VISION Studio does not host your videos on a public server.

Can I use content I did not create?

Only if you own the rights or have the owner's permission. You remain responsible for complying with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

How do I disconnect my account or delete my data?

Use Disconnect TikTok inside VISION Studio, or remove the app from your TikTok security settings. Locally stored tokens are deleted. Prepared clips and publication history can be deleted from the application, and you can also email measaidi953@gmail.com.

Is VISION Studio affiliated with TikTok?

No. VISION Studio is an independent creator tool that uses TikTok's public developer APIs under TikTok's developer terms.

What platforms are supported?

VISION Studio is a desktop application. This website provides information, support and the legal documents for the application.
