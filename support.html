Support

Help with account authorization, publication status, privacy and deletion.

Connect or disconnect TikTok

Use the TikTok connection control inside VISION Studio. TikTok displays the permissions requested before authorization. Disconnecting removes the locally stored authorization tokens. You can also revoke access from your TikTok account under Settings → Security → Apps and services.

A post is still processing

TikTok can take several minutes to process uploaded media. VISION Studio keeps the item in the processing state until TikTok reports success or failure, and shows the reason for any failure.

A post failed

Common causes are a video that exceeds TikTok's duration or size limits, a daily posting limit reached on the account, or a privacy level that is not available to the account. VISION Studio shows TikTok's response so the clip can be adjusted and resubmitted.

Delete local data

Prepared clips, scheduled items and publication history can be deleted from within VISION Studio. To request assistance with deletion or revocation, contact the address below.
