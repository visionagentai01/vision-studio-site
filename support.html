Support

Help with account authorization, publication status, privacy and deletion.

Connect or disconnect TikTok

Use the TikTok connection control inside VISION Studio. TikTok displays the permissions requested before authorization. Disconnecting removes the locally stored authorization tokens.

A post is still processing

TikTok can take several minutes to process uploaded media. VISION Studio keeps the item in processing state until TikTok reports success or failure.

Delete local data

Prepared clips and publication history can be deleted from the local VISION Studio installation. To request assistance with deletion or revocation, contact the address below.
